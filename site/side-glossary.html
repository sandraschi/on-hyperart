Side - Glossary — on-hyperart

Appoggiatura — Italian: "to lean." An expressive musical ornament where an unprepared, emotionally accented non-harmonic tone delays the expected harmonic pitch, resolving downward or upward by step. The acoustic mechanism relies on withholding harmonic resolution to heighten emotional yield. Ch III.

Classical.choice — The Axiom of Choice in Lean's formal type-theory kernel. While constructive logic requires every mathematical object to be furnished with an explicit algorithmic recipe, Classical.choice permits non-constructive assertions of existence without a witness. Used in Side-hypermath to audit whether derivations are constructed or discovered.

Comping — Composite vocal tracking: the DAW-era studio practice of assembling a single, seamless "master" performance by splicing syllables, breaths, and micro-phrases across dozens of recorded human takes. Pre-AI precedent for Threshold A; severed the concept of the single unbroken take decades before neural synthesis. Ch IV, Ch VI.

Compromise envelope — historic iron triangle: narrative depth vs visual velocity vs scale. Ch IV. Liquidated at zero marginal cost.

Deniable archetype — latent-space face/voice between established icons (e.g. granite jaw + glottal anxiety) with zero liability. Ch VI/VIII + side.

Folk-art demotion — biological art respected for limitation, dwarfed in density/power. Calligraphy precedent. Ch IX.

Formant — Distinct acoustic resonance peaks in the mammalian vocal tract determined by throat and mouth geometry. Specific frequency relationships between the first and second formants ($F_1$ and $F_2$) transmit non-verbal distress, panic, and grief signals across all mammalian biology prior to semantic processing. Ch I, Ch III, Ch VI.

Itano circus — missile-swarm animation choreography (Macross). Ch VIII; use for kinetic-dimension claims only with dated artifact.

Kanada smear / impact frame — limited-animation distortion techniques. Ch IV/VIII; never generic "animation". Exaggerated perspective warping held for 1–2 frames to convey kinetic violence beyond mechanical motion blur.

LDDO (Low-Density Derivative Output) — Institutional/bureaucratic euphemism for "slop": high-volume, low-effort synthetic or human output exhibiting near-zero information entropy, repetitive tropes, and unedited execution. Side-on-slop.

Last Take / Frame / Face / Cut / Venue — Thresholds A-E. Defined in Ch VI; listed here only as index.

Orthogonal hypercinema — maxing all dials at once: Nobel-density script + sakuga kinetics + 72-hour run. Ch VIII.

Sakuga — peak-intensity animated craft (animator-credited cuts). Ch IV/V.

String vacua / String landscape — The enormous ensemble (traditionally estimated at $\sim 10^{500}$) of theoretically stable configurations that extra-dimensional string theory can settle into. Each "vacuum" represents a distinct local minimum of energy with its own fundamental forces, particles, and cosmological constants. Side-hypermath.

Sturgeon's Law — "Ninety percent of everything is crud" (Theodore Sturgeon, 1953). In this book: the biological invariant that pins human cultural production to a fixed 90% mediocrity baseline across centuries, contrasted with the moving synthetic gradient where pipeline pruning, reasoning kernels, and automated checkers drive the error rate from 90% down to 80%, 70%, and lower. Side-on-slop.

Verifier clock — the domain's test latency sets its collapse speed. Type checker: ms. Kinetics: ms. Simulator: minutes. Audience: hours at population scale. Organism: years. Art fell first because its verifier is a mammal who answers surveys. Side-hypermath; wired into Ch I.

Validated novelty — growth in the reachable set of consequences under a checker, as opposed to free Shannon information. The honest form of the "verified recombination" argument. Side-hypermath.

World-alienation — Arendt's objection, via Lepore: a world made entirely by machines leaves humans encountering only themselves in disguise. The strongest counter-thesis the book faces. Side-lepore-counter-thesis.

Warning-as-recipe — the corpus cannot distinguish a cautionary tale from an instruction manual; the loss function has no column for against. Ch II (Lepore ch. 12).
