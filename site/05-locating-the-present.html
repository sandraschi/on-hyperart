What has NOT happened (say it plainly)

No verified unbroken multi-day synthetic narrative has shipped to a paying audience without a biological cut. No lead screen likeness has been conclusively shown to be a deniable archetype with dated provenance. No award body has ruled a fully synthetic score eligible or ineligible with published reasoning. No BCI narrative master exists outside lab demos. These are not "not yet" as prophecy. They are "not evidenced" as discipline. A ladder with silent gaps is a horoscope; this one lists its holes on purpose so the next revision can fill or move them.

Which rung are we on

Music: Bands I-III crossed, Band IV in progress — convergence drafts circulate, full monograph-density albums at peak production do not yet have dated provenance. Voice: Band II-III boundary — chimeras ship as novelty and placeholder, the Last Take has not verifiably fallen for lead dramatic performance. Image: Band II-IV spread — peak-intensity test footage weekly, sustained 100-minute convergence feature with dated pipeline: not evidenced. Exhibition: Band V in motion — the window is custom, not law. Immersion: Band VI drafting — demos, not masters.

In takeover-book coordinates this is the equivalent of rung 13-14: the audit floor is cracking under us. We can still, barely, reconstruct why a given released artifact sounds and looks the way it does. The week we cannot — the week the provenance chain goes from "fatigued mixer approved it" to "nobody can say what approved it" — is the Audit Floor's aesthetic twin, and it will not announce itself. It will look like a very productive Tuesday.
