# VI. Thresholds as Attachment Points

## Why thresholds, not rungs

"Withhold synthesis as needed" is not a policy. It is the shape of a policy
that has nowhere to be written down — Pachocki's alien-mind phrasing (OpenAI, Sept 2026: commitments to withhold scaling "as needed", with no threshold to attach them to ) applies
word for word to the studio that promises restraint without naming the line.
A rung describes. A threshold binds: do not cross without an audit regime, a
named owner, and a verification the rival can observe. Kahn's thresholds had
names crews could remember in the dark — Don't Rock the Boat, No Nuclear Use,
Central Sanctuary. Ours need the same memorability, because the crossing will
happen at 02:00 with a fatigued mixer at the desk, and nobody reads footnotes
at 02:00.

Each threshold below: definition, crossing test, owner, precedent. A threshold
nobody owns is a wish. Wishes are Chapter VII's subject, not this one's.

## A — The Last Take (vocal)

**Definition:** the last shipped lead vocal whose credited performer sang that line, in some take. **Crossing test:** the performer never sang it — no session producible in which they did. Comping[^comping], tuning, and punch-ins stay human; substitution does not. (The naive version — one room, one unbroken take — fell decades ago to multitrack comping and pitch correction: Cher, "Believe", 1998 ; the DAW era made the unbroken take a choice, then a rarity. This threshold is drawn past them on purpose.)
**Owner:** labels and unions first, statute second . **Precedent:**
the temp that stayed. Every mixer alive has kept a scratch vocal because the
"real" session never beat it for feel. Threshold A is the day the scratch
stops being the exception and becomes the master, and nobody writes it down
because writing it down would require a meeting about what a singer is.

The terror here is not replacement. It is continuity: the audience will not
notice. The formant does not check provenance; the listener's beliefs do — and beliefs are trainable.

## B — The Last Frame (visual labor)

**Definition:** the last shipped cut whose key animation a studio can attribute to a named human. **Crossing test:**
the studio cannot name a key animator (genga) per cut, on demand — from credit sheets, not memory. (The naive version — the hand-drawn frame — fell in 1990 to Disney CAPS ink-and-paint, *The Rescuers Down Under* , and CG in-betweening finished the job. This threshold is about authorship records, not graphite.)
**Owner:** studios and unions ; award
bodies as secondary witnesses. **Precedent:** previz lock. The previsualization
cut, once a sketch, became the film before casting finished — the sketch that
ate the shoot. The Last Frame is previz's final victory: the drawing that was
never a drawing, only a prompt's confident child.

Exhilaration, honestly logged: every frame at Redline intensity without the
seven-year hemorrhage. The end of the suicide premium. Nobody has to sleep
under the desk again. The price is that nobody can point at a smear and name
the wrist. Craft becomes curation — the gardener's job from Chapter IV, now
with a union steward watching the garden get gardened.

## C — The Last Face (likeness)

**Definition:** the last lead likeness that is a contracted human face.
**Crossing test:** the deniable archetype — a face the audience reads as
star-grade, the lab reads as interpolation between icons, and the lawyer reads
as no one. Granite jaw remembered from somewhere, glottal anxiety borrowed
from nowhere actionable. **Owner:** right-of-publicity regime, and that is the
problem .
**Precedent:** Face-equity flight — talent converting contracted-likeness value into asset classes no pipeline prices
into unburnable assets while the pipeline still prices faces .

This is the most legally exposed threshold: discrete statutes cannot bind continuous space. The law names persons; the
latent walks between them. Every named face is an island; the model is the sea.
You cannot sue the sea for resembling your island from a distance. Threshold C
will be crossed deniably, repeatedly, and the first lawsuit will settle without
admission because admission would require explaining interpolation to a jury at
billable rates. The brake that needs a jury's intuition about high-dimensional
geometry is not a brake. It is a seminar.

## D — The Last Cut (exhibition)

**Definition:** the last work with a fixed runtime at all. **Crossing test:** two viewers cannot have seen 'the same film' — generated, extended, or personalised per viewer, so runtime is a property of the session, not the work. (Length alone fell long ago: Warhol's *Empire* (1964, ~8 h), Tarr's *Satantango* (1994, ~7.5 h), Marclay's *The Clock* (2010, 24 h)  — and serial TV demoted the 120-minute law to custom. This threshold is drawn past length: identity of the work, not duration.)
**Owner:** theaters, streamers, award bodies . **Precedent:** the window's corpse. The
90-day window became 45, became day-and-date, became custom, became nostalgia.
Nobody voted; the custom just stopped being enforceable when the living room
out-rendered the multiplex and the catalog outweighed the premiere.

The exhilaration is real and should not be laundered: institutional decay
tracked across centuries without cutting away — the thing the novel could do
and cinema never could, because cinema had to let you pee. The terror is the
same object from the other side: nobody can watch it either, so "premiere"
becomes "deployment." Culture as infrastructure update. You do not attend the
72-hour work. You live near it while it runs. (The fixed long form is the rehearsal; the threshold is the unfixing.)

## E — The Last Venue (immersion)

**Definition:** the last master mixed for flat glass. **Crossing test:** the
spatial/BCI master ships first and the cinema cut is labeled downmix — the
word "downmix" appearing in dated production notes. **Owner:** device makers
and medical regulators, the strangest owners on this list and the most honest:
the only brakes left are the ones that can seize hardware or deny a trial.
**Precedent:** stereo-to-mono in reverse. Every prior format kept the old
venue as the master and the new one as garnish. Threshold E inverts the garnish.

## How to use this chapter

Photocopy it. That is the design brief — one page per threshold on the wall
of every cutting room, dub stage, and commissioning desk. Not as decoration.
As the thing the tired person at 02:00 can read without waking the lawyer:
have we crossed A? Who do I call? What do they need to see? If the answer to
the third question is "nothing observable," the threshold is already crossed
and this chapter becomes archaeology. Archaeology is also useful. Someone
should date the ruins.

## Fleet link

Takeover-book's Threshold B (Audit Floor) is "no human institution can
independently verify a claim about model behavior." Hyperwar's thresholds are
export chokepoints and hotlines that work at machine speed. Ours are the same
shape in the mirror: A-E mark where verification of human provenance stops
being available. Four books, one sentence: the brake must be ownable before
the crossing, or it is literature afterwards. This chapter aims to be hardware.
