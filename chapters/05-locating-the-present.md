Music: Bands I-III crossed, Band IV in progress — convergence drafts circulate,
full monograph-density albums at peak production do not yet have dated
provenance. Voice: Band II-III boundary — chimeras ship as novelty and
placeholder, the Last Take has not verifiably fallen for lead dramatic
performance. Image: Band II-IV spread — peak-intensity test footage weekly,
sustained 100-minute convergence feature with dated pipeline: not evidenced.
Exhibition: Band V in motion — the window is custom, not law. Immersion:
Band VI drafting — demos, not masters.

In takeover-book coordinates this is the equivalent of rung 13-14: the audit
floor is cracking under us. We can still, barely, reconstruct why a given
released artifact sounds and looks the way it does. The week we cannot — the
week the provenance chain goes from "fatigued mixer approved it" to "nobody
can say what approved it" — is Threshold B's aesthetic twin, and it will not
announce itself. It will look like a very productive Tuesday.
