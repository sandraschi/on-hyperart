# PRD — on-hyperart

**Status:** construction v0.1 (2026-10-11). Private repo. Audience: principals
who commission, publish, or insure culture — not the general public first.
**Trilogy position:** complements on-hyperwar (kinetic/strategic) and
on-ai-takeover (systemic/economic). Focus: semantic, sensory, aesthetic singularity.

## Problem

Art discourse sorts by capability ("is it creative?") or by moral panic framing, which cannot be dated and cannot be acted on. What operators need is ordering by **reversibility of human gatekeeping**, where each step is an observable institutional fact with a date, and each threshold names who can still attach a brake and how.

## Thesis (one sentence)

We will not lose the aesthetic monopoly in an explosion; we already lost it
one irreversible compromise at a time — iron-triangle trade-offs liquidated,
biological ceilings breached, face-as-asset evaporated — each with a dated
artifact and an attachment point.

## Design constraints (from CONTRIBUTING, normative)

- Rule 1 Observability: every claim dateable/checkable; private pipelines
  excluded until publicly evidenced; intentions need quoted speech.
- Rule 2 Hazard: institutional facts + governance criteria only; no
  likeness-targeted clone guides, no filter-defeat detail; CSAM-adjacent
  content cut on sight.
- Style: plain declarative, dates first, Kahn-flat; no skynet/terminator;
  no "soul"/"lived experience" as argument-stoppers. Domain precision:
  sakuga, impact frames, Kanada smears, formants, glottal fry — not
  generic "animation" / "good sound".
- Multi-model debate, human judge; `Co-Authored-By:` trailers where used.

## Tooling (shipped, this repo — ported from sisters)

- `tools/declaim.py` + `just declaim`: markdown to speech (Gemini if key,
  else Windows SAPI). Read-aloud gate for every chapter.
- `tools/lorecheck.py` + `just lore`: chapters I-X coverage, dates,
  forbidden terms. Baseline 2026-10-11: 0/10, none forbidden.
- Issues 1-5 to draft after `gh auth login` (Farinelli verification,
  ELVIS/AB-1836 cite, seiyuu-exhaustion cite, theatrical-window table,
  T_n-style generation-time box for render cost).
